# Changelog

Fixes collect under **Unreleased** on the `next` branch. Nothing reaches users until Shovon says to release.

## Unreleased (next: v4.0)
- Calmer messages: rename, delete and "discard changes?" are Space-style cards instead of browser boxes; tips are short cards at the top with one button that does the fix; the pairing pill is black and white.
- New script opens an empty paste box straight away (rename it with the pencil).
- Hover notes: rest the mouse on a control's name to see what it does.
- Calmer right panel: one thin line per section, keyboard shortcuts behind one row. Side panels are 15% bigger on laptops and desktops.
- First visit: the prompter opens with a short welcome that reads like a real script. Press Space to see it scroll; Edit script to paste your own.
- Scripts list: newest first, with the date and word count under each name.
- Make it yours: click the logo, pick your own logo or a photo, press Save. Saved on this device.
- What's new: after an update, a short card shows what changed (once). Click the version, bottom left, to see it again. The update notice is one short line with a close button.
- Version moved to the bottom line ("Space Prompter v… © shovonix"); the name beside the logo is bigger.
- Send feedback: bottom of the left panel (Bug, Idea or Love it, with screenshots or video).
- How to use: the ? beside Controls opens a few short steps.
- Keys list in Controls matches Space Cam; ↑ ↓ change the speed in Dual Monitor too.
- Every popup blurs the background the same way.
- Light mode: the sun / moon button in the Controls panel and on the paste page. The Stage window always stays black for the prompter glass.
- The installed app always opens the newest version, and checks for updates when you click back into it.
- No flash of the wrong page on reload.
- Pasting from Google Docs, Word or a web page keeps your paragraphs and empty lines exactly. Extra blank lines add space on the prompter.
